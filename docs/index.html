ble·sign·beacongood-trouble

Bluetooth Low Energy sit-in beacon. Broadcast arbitrary sign-messages as raw BLE advertisements on any ~$5 ESP32. "Choosing to be seen is the whole idea of a sit-in."

Expressive RF Presence · Speech, Not a Blockade

This project is designed to make civil presence countable and visible in the RF spectrum. Unlike RF jammers or deauthentication attacks that deny spectrum to others, ble-sign-beacon operates strictly within regulatory compliance: low transmit power (-12 dBm), ordinary 100 ms advertising intervals, and locally-administered OUIs. It does not interfere with the band—it speaks into it.

01 · arbitrary ble packet broadcast: deeper than a beacon

Commercial Bluetooth beacons (such as Apple iBeacon or Google Eddystone) lock transmission into rigid, proprietary payloads that advertise a fixed UUID, major/minor integers, or a URL.

ble-sign-beacon bypasses these limits by crafting raw, arbitrary GAP (Generic Access Profile) advertising frames directly against the ESP32 radio stack:

  • Arbitrary Text Payloads: Encapsulates user messages directly into Complete Local Name (AD Type 0x09) fields.
  • Custom Manufacturer Data: Injects arbitrary byte sequences under experimental Company Identifier 0xFFFF (AD Type 0xFF).
  • Locally-Administered OUI Rotation: Rotates lower MAC addresses on every broadcast cycle while preserving a fixed, honest community prefix (02:47:54 → ASCII "GT" for Good Trouble) with the locally-administered bit set.
  • Non-Connectable Broadcast: Advertises in ADV_NONCONN_IND mode, ensuring smartphones and BLE scanners hear the packet without attempting or allowing Bluetooth pairing.

02 · the good trouble protocol, ethics & prohibited behavior specification

Protest technology requires strict ethical and technical discipline to avoid becoming a public nuisance, causing denial-of-service, or endangering fellow citizens. ble-sign-beacon exists to establish expressive, peaceful presence in the RF spectrum—not to engage in electronic warfare or spectrum sabotage.

The Non-Doppelganging Imperative · Why Identity Spoofing is Unethical

Doppelganging is the antithesis of civil disobedience. In a sit-in, activists willingly offer their presence as moral witness. If an operator sniffs a real civilian's MAC address and clones it while broadcasting protest slogans, any surveillance system (whether an automated automated listener like sentinel-node or a municipal dragnet) logs that the innocent civilian was the source of the broadcast. In a criminalized or hostile jurisdiction, this manufactures false forensic evidence, exposing bystanders to unwarranted interrogation, arrest, or geofence subpoenas. True "Good Trouble" speaks under its own collective banner (02:47:54); it never hides behind a civilian shield.

04 · deployment options

Option A — Static Firmware (good_trouble.ino)

Cycles through an array of hardcoded protest chants every 2,000 ms, randomizing the lower 3 octets of the MAC address on each rotation: 

static const char* MESSAGES[] = {
  "GOOD_TROUBLE", "CONSENT_DENIED", "I_AM_NOT_DATA",
  "NO_TRACKING",   "STOP_WATCHING",   "TRACK_THIS"
};
static const uint8_t TROUBLE_OUI[3] = { 0x02, 0x47, 0x54 }; // 'GT', locally administered

Option B — Transient Captive Portal (good_trouble_config.ino)

On first boot (or when the BOOT button is held), the ESP32 opens an ephemeral SoftAP named good-trouble-setup with a captive DNS portal. The user joins from their phone, saves their custom message into NVS flash, and the firmware permanently kills the Wi-Fi stack (WiFi.mode(WIFI_OFF)), transitioning purely into low-power BLE broadcast.
